# Traceo — Product Context

## What is this product?
Traceo is an internal JV Facility & Chronology Management System for tracking financing facilities (JV companies, JV land, individual loans). It replaces manual Excel/Word workflows.

## Who uses it?
- **Admin** — Full system management
- **Pengurus (Manager)** — Manage facilities and follow-ups, export reports
- **Pegawai Susulan (Follow-up Officer)** — Add follow-up records on assigned facilities
- **Viewer** — Read-only access to facility data

## Core jobs-to-be-done
1. Record and track JV financing facilities with key financial data
2. Log follow-up visits/activities (date + notes + photos) chronologically
3. Generate formatted Kronologi (chronology) reports → export to Word (.docx) and PDF
4. Monitor arrears and facility status at a glance

## Tone & Voice
- Professional, precise, administrative — this is a business tool
- Interface language: English (UI labels) + Malay (content/data)
- No marketing copy. Everything should be direct and functional.
- CTAs should describe the action precisely: "Jana kronologi", "Tambah susulan", "Eksport Word"

## Key constraints
- Must work well on desktop (primary) and mobile (for field officers uploading photos)
- Minimize unnecessary data fetching — officers in the field may have slow connections
- Data accuracy over animation — this is for financial record-keeping
